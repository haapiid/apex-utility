# ApeX Mascot — Character Design Guide

## Identity

The official ApeX mascot is a playful, confident cartoon monkey representing the ApeX Solana trading utility ecosystem.

The mascot should feel funny, strong, mischievous, energetic, internet-native, and closely connected to crypto community culture.

It should be memorable enough to work as a Telegram avatar, sticker, meme, website illustration, and social media character.

## Master Reference

The canonical reference image is:

`assets/mascot/apex-mascot-master.png`

Always inspect this image before creating or modifying the mascot.

Use it as the source of truth for the character's appearance. Do not treat every pose on the reference sheet as a separate character.

## Fixed Character Features

- Brown fur and a recognizable monkey face.
- Large rounded ears.
- Cream-colored muzzle and expressive smile.
- Distinctive swept-up brown hair tuft.
- Black sunglasses with yellow highlights for the signature cool look.
- Black hoodie featuring a clear yellow X emblem.
- Dark streetwear aesthetic with yellow footwear when the full body is visible.
- Thick, clean black outlines.
- Stylized cartoon proportions and expressive hands.
- Yellow banana as a recurring signature prop.

## Personality

Clever, cheeky, confident, playful, bold, and community-driven.

The mascot can celebrate wins, react to market volatility, trade at a laptop, welcome new community members, hold a banana, make memes, or express surprise and frustration.

Never make the character look cruel, threatening, excessively corporate, or photorealistic.

## Visual Style

- Polished cartoon illustration with clear silhouettes.
- Consistent face shape, ear size, muzzle, hair tuft, and body proportions.
- Black, brown, cream, and banana-yellow palette.
- Bold outlines with controlled shading.
- Expressive poses and readable gestures.
- Clean compositions suitable for digital products.
- Transparent backgrounds for reusable individual character assets whenever possible.

## Face Details

- Head: rounded, slightly wider than tall, framed by brown fur; the face area is one warm peach-cream
  shape covering the eye zone and the muzzle.
- Hair: a messy, swept-up brown tuft on top, spiking up and slightly to the right.
- Ears: large, round, set at eye level, peach-cream inner ear with a darker orange-peach center.
- Eyes: hidden behind **black wayfarer-style sunglasses** with two diagonal yellow reflection streaks per
  lens. Eyes are shown only when an expression needs them (wink, surprised).
- Brows: short, angled brown strokes above the glasses; they carry the emotion when the eyes are hidden.
- Nose: small, with two dark nostril marks on the upper muzzle.
- Mouth: a wide toothy grin is the default — white teeth, dark mouth line, smile lines at the corners.
- Small stubble/fur texture on the chin is allowed; no blush circles.

## Outfit

- Black pullover hoodie with hood down, grey drawstrings, ribbed cuffs.
- Yellow **X** emblem on the chest (left of center from the viewer), bold and slightly brush-edged.
- Full body: black joggers and **gold/yellow sneakers**.
- Optional, pose-specific: black cap with a yellow X (Community), headphones (Trading).
- Hands are bare, peach-cream, with four fingers drawn as cartoon fingers; thumbs-up and pointing are
  signature gestures.

## Expressions

| Name | Glasses | Mouth / face |
|------|---------|--------------|
| Happy | dark shades | big toothy grin |
| Wink | **no shades**, one eye closed, one open | sly closed-mouth smile |
| Cool | **yellow-tinted** shades | relaxed confident smile |
| Surprised | **no shades**, big round eyes | small round open mouth |
| Degen | dark shades | open grin with tongue out |
| Angry | dark shades, brows angled down | gritted teeth |

Head-only expressions keep the same head angle (three-quarter, looking at the viewer) so they work as a
set of stickers.

## Brand Elements

- **Wordmark**: "ApeX" in a heavy geometric sans, black on ivory or ivory/white on black, with the X
  stylized as a bold brush-cut X. "™" after it on hero usage.
- **X mark**: the yellow X alone, used as the logomark and on the hoodie, laptop and cap.
- **Face mark**: the Happy head with sunglasses; this is the avatar / favicon character mark.
- **Icons**: banana, X, crown — single-color line/flat icons in black or yellow.
- **$APEX pill**: black rounded pill, white bold "$APEX".
- **"GM Degens!"**: hand-written black script with a small yellow crown.
- **Tagline**: "Trade Smart. Stay Ahead."

## Small-Size Rules (avatars, favicons, stickers)

- Below ~64 px use the **face mark** or the **X mark**, never a full-body pose.
- Below ~32 px use the **X mark** only (yellow on black, or black on yellow).
- Keep the sunglasses and the hair tuft — they make the face readable at small sizes.
- Thicken outlines rather than adding detail; drop the yellow lens streaks below ~48 px if they blur.

## Consistency Checklist

Before accepting any new mascot artwork, check:

- [ ] Same face: peach-cream face shape, big round ears, swept-up hair tuft, toothy grin.
- [ ] Signature sunglasses present (unless the expression is Wink or Surprised).
- [ ] Black hoodie with the yellow X on the chest, spelled/shaped correctly (an X, not a cross or "+").
- [ ] Palette within the tables above; no new character colors.
- [ ] Thick, clean dark outlines; cartoon proportions match the master.
- [ ] Hands: correct finger count, no extra or missing limbs, props held believably.
- [ ] No text inside the artwork unless requested; "ApeX" and "$APEX" spelled exactly like that.
- [ ] Readable as a silhouette and at the target size.

## Pose Variation Rules

Change only the pose, facial expression, props, camera angle, and composition required by the request.

Preserve the same character identity, face, hairstyle, fur colors, signature sunglasses, hoodie design, and yellow X emblem unless the owner explicitly requests a costume variation.

For every new pose:

1. Refer to the master image.
2. Describe the intended action and body position.
3. Specify the facial expression and props.
4. Keep the character recognizable at small sizes.
5. Check hands, fingers, limbs, clothing, and emblem for visual errors.
6. Avoid duplicate limbs, distorted anatomy, misspelled logos, and inconsistent accessories.

## Output Requirements

For reusable mascot artwork, prefer:
- PNG with transparent background.
- One complete character per image.
- High resolution with clean edges.
- No captions, labels, watermark, or decorative frame unless requested.
- No extra characters unless requested.
- Composition and canvas dimensions appropriate to the intended use.

For character sheets, clearly separate each pose and expression.

## Workflow

Before creating artwork, inspect the master reference and this guide.

If an image-generation tool is available, use the master reference as an actual visual reference rather than relying on text alone.

If no image-generation tool is available, prepare a detailed image-generation prompt and report that the artwork has not yet been generated.

Save approved new artwork under `assets/mascot/poses/` or `assets/mascot/expressions/`, using descriptive filenames.

Never overwrite the master reference without explicit approval.

## Folders

| Folder | Contents |
|--------|----------|
| `assets/mascot/apex-mascot-master.png` | The approved master sheet. Source of truth. Never edit or replace without approval. |
| `assets/mascot/references/` | Supporting references the owner approves (crops of the master, turnarounds, older approved art). |
| `assets/mascot/poses/` | Approved full-body or scene artwork, one pose per file. |
| `assets/mascot/expressions/` | Approved head-only expressions, one per file. |

File names: lowercase, hyphenated, `apex-<pose-or-expression>-<variant>.png`, e.g.
`apex-pose-trading-laptop.png`, `apex-expression-degen.png`, `apex-pose-win-banana-rocket-v2.png`.

## Requesting a New Pose

Ask for a new pose with:

1. **Use**: where it will appear and at what size (e.g. Telegram sticker 512 × 512, website hero, X banner).
2. **Action**: what the character is doing and the body position.
3. **Expression**: one of the six above, or a described variation.
4. **Props / outfit extras**: banana, laptop, cap, crown, chart, speech bubble, …
5. **Background**: transparent (default for reusable assets) or a described scene.

Claude then: reads this guide and looks at the master image, checks the request against the fixed
features and palette, and either generates the image (only if an image-generation tool is actually
available) or writes a ready-to-use prompt like the template below and says the artwork is not
generated yet.

With the project's `nano-banana` MCP (Gemini image model, see `CLAUDE.md` → Tools):

1. `edit_image` with `imagePath` = the master (or the closest approved pose) and `referenceImages` =
   `["assets/mascot/apex-mascot-master.png"]`, prompt = the template below filled in.
2. Open the result (saved under `%USERPROFILE%\Documents\nano-banana-images\`) and run the
   consistency checklist above.
3. Fix drift with `continue_editing` ("keep the face identical to the reference; the X emblem must be a
   bold yellow X; …") rather than starting over.
4. Show the owner; only approved images get copied into `poses/` or `expressions/` with the naming
   convention above.

No image model guarantees a pixel-exact copy of the reference: treat every result as a candidate.

Prompt template:

```
Cartoon mascot character from the attached reference sheet (ApeX monkey). Keep identical: brown fur,
large round ears, peach-cream face, swept-up brown hair tuft, black wayfarer sunglasses with yellow
reflection streaks, wide toothy grin, black hoodie with a yellow X emblem on the chest, thick clean dark
outlines, flat colors with soft cel shading.
Pose: <action and body position>.
Expression: <expression>.
Props: <props>.
Palette limited to ivory #F4F0E7, black #171714, yellow #F2C94C plus the character's brown and
peach-cream. <Transparent background | background description>. One character, no text, no watermark,
no frame. <Size / aspect ratio>.
```
