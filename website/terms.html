Trades and automation

Before a manual trade ApeX shows a quote with the fee, the estimated and minimum amount you receive and the price impact. The final result depends on the market when the transaction lands and can differ from the quote within your slippage setting. A trade can fail; when it does, no ApeX fee is charged, but the network fee may still be spent.

Automated actions run on our servers and depend on market data, routers and networks we don’t control. We don’t promise that an order fills at its trigger price, that a snipe gets in first, that a copy trade mirrors every move or that anti-rug sells before a price collapses. Orders can trigger late, at a worse price, or not at all. You are responsible for the actions you set up until you cancel them.

Safety scans and labels such as “no red flags” are automated checks on third-party data. They are not a guarantee that a token is safe.

ApeX charges a fee on each successful trade: currently 0.5% for manual trades, limit orders, DCA and copy trades, and 1% for snipes, including pump.fun rules. The fee is taken inside the swap and shown before you confirm a manual trade. Network fees, priority fees and MEV-protection tips go to the network or its validators, not to ApeX. We may change fees; the fee shown when a trade is quoted is the one that applies to it.
