Questions

Which tokens can I trade?

Any token with liquidity on Solana, Ethereum, BSC, Base, HyperEVM, MegaETH or Robinhood Chain. Paste its address: the bot finds the chain and checks a route exists. New launches work once they have a pool, or snipe them before.

Do I need Telegram?

No. ApeX Pro runs in your browser and you can log in with Phantom, MetaMask or Google. If you also use the bot, link the two and you get one account with the same wallets, orders and rewards.

Who holds my wallet?

The bot stores your wallets’ keys encrypted so it can sign the trades you confirm. You can export them at any time. Keep only your trading funds in them, not your savings.

How is the revenue share calculated?

After the referral cut, 40% of every fee goes to a holder pool for each chain. Every 24 hours it is split between everyone holding at least 50 $APEX in their ApeX wallets, by how much they hold, and paid in the coin the fees came in. /revenue shows the live pool.

How do referrals work?

Share your link from /referral. You earn 25% of the trading fees of everyone who joins through it, for as long as they trade, and claim it in /rewards.

When does $APEX launch?

Not yet. Until then the holder pool, referral earnings and your cashback keep adding up, so nothing from the first trades is lost.

What happens if a trade fails?

You keep your coins apart from the small network fee, and no ApeX fee is charged. The bot tells you why it failed, for example the price moved more than your slippage.

Is this financial advice?

No. ApeX is a tool. Memecoins are volatile and can go to zero. Only trade what you can afford to lose.
