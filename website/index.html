Your first trade in four taps

  1. Open the bot and tap Create Wallet. You get a Solana wallet that also works in Phantom.
  2. Deposit SOL to the address the bot shows you.
  3. Paste a token address in the chat. You see price, market cap and what you hold.
  4. Tap Buy, then Confirm. The bot shows the fee, the minimum you’ll receive and the price impact before anything is sent.

Where your 1% goes

Every trade pays a 1% fee. ApeX keeps 60%. The other 40% is a reward pool for the community, split between token holders and the traders who generate the volume.

Built to keep your keys yours

  • Private keys are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they’re stored.
  • A message with a private key is deleted as soon as the bot reads it.
  • Every trade and withdrawal waits for your confirmation.
  • Export your key anytime and use the same wallet in Phantom.
  • The bot only works in private chats, never in groups.
  • ApeX will never DM you first or ask for your key.

Questions

Which tokens can I trade?

Any Solana token with liquidity on a DEX that Jupiter routes through, including new launches once they have a pool. Paste the token’s mint address to see if a route exists.

Who holds my wallet?

The bot stores your wallet’s key encrypted so it can sign the trades you confirm. You can export it at any time. Keep only your trading funds in it, not your savings.

How is the revenue share calculated?

40% of all fees goes to a reward pool. 70% of that pool is split between $APEX holders by holdings, and 30% between active traders by their share of the last 24 hours of volume. /revenue shows the live numbers.

What happens if a trade fails?

You keep your SOL apart from the small network fee, and no ApeX fee is charged. The bot tells you why it failed, for example the price moved more than 5%.

Is this financial advice?

No. ApeX is a tool. Memecoins are volatile and can go to zero. Only trade what you can afford to lose.
