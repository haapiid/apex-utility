Privacy Policy

This policy explains what ApeX Utility (“ApeX”, “we”) collects when you use the Telegram bot, the ApeX Pro web app and this website, why, who it is shared with and how long it is kept. We collect only what ApeX needs to work. We don’t sell your data and we don’t use advertising trackers.

What we collect

Account. Your Telegram user ID if you use the bot. Your first name from Telegram is used to greet you but not stored. If you sign in to ApeX Pro without Telegram: the wallet address you sign in with, or your Google account ID and email address.

Wallets. The addresses of your ApeX wallets and their private keys, encrypted with AES-256-GCM.

Activity. Your trades (token, amounts, fees, transaction hashes), positions, limit orders, DCA plans, snipes, pump.fun rules, copy targets, watchlist, tracked wallets, notifications, settings, referral link and rewards, claims and burns.

Security. For ApeX Pro sessions: a hashed session token, your browser’s user agent and when the session was created and last used. A hash of your ApeX Pro PIN, never the PIN itself.

Technical. Your IP address is processed to limit request rates and may appear in server logs together with the time and the page requested. Error logs record the failing request, not your keys.

We do not ask for your real name, phone number, ID documents or payment cards.

How we use it

  • To run ApeX: sign you in, carry out the trades and automated actions you set up, and show your wallets, positions and history in both the bot and ApeX Pro.
  • To calculate and pay fees, referral earnings, cashback and revenue share.
  • To keep ApeX and your account secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To send you notifications about your orders and account, in the bot and ApeX Pro.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We process this data to provide the service you asked for, for our legitimate interest in keeping it secure, and where the law requires it.

Blockchains are public

Every trade, transfer and claim is recorded on a public blockchain with your wallet address. Anyone can see it, and nobody, including us, can delete it. Copy trading and the wallet tracker only read data that is already public on-chain.

Who we share it with

We share only what each service needs to do its job:

  • Telegram delivers bot messages.
  • Routers and transaction relays (Jupiter, KyberSwap, Jito, Flashbots Protect, PancakeSwap MEV Guard) and blockchain RPC providers receive the wallet address, tokens and amounts of a trade to quote and send it.
  • Market and safety data (DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, GoPlus, PumpPortal, Jupiter) receive the token and wallet addresses being looked up.
  • Google confirms your identity when you choose Google sign-in.
  • Our hosting provider runs the servers that store the data.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect users and ApeX from fraud.

These pages load fonts from Google Fonts and scripts from cdnjs (this website) and jsDelivr (ApeX Pro’s chart). Those providers see your IP address when your browser fetches the files.

Cookies and browser storage

ApeX Pro sets one cookie: your sign-in session (HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, valid for 30 days or until you log out). It also keeps your selected chain, chart timeframe and any referral code you arrived with in your browser’s local storage. This website sets no cookies. We use no analytics or advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

  • Account, wallet and trade data: for as long as your account exists, and afterwards only as long as the law requires.
  • ApeX Pro sessions: 30 days, or until you log out.
  • Wallet tracker events: 7 days.
  • One-time sign-in codes and challenges: minutes, until they are used or expire.
  • Server logs: a limited time, for security and troubleshooting.

Security

Private keys are encrypted before they are stored, session tokens and PINs are stored only as hashes, and ApeX Pro is served over HTTPS with strict browser security headers. Messages that contain a private key are deleted from the chat as soon as the bot reads them. No system is perfectly secure: keep only trading funds in ApeX wallets and protect your Telegram account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Indonesia’s Personal Data Protection Law (UU No. 27/2022), you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, get a copy of it, or object to how we use it. You can already export your keys, remove wallets and cancel automated actions yourself. For anything else, contact us from the account concerned. Before deleting an account, withdraw your funds or export your keys: once it is deleted, we can’t recover them. Data on public blockchains can’t be deleted.

Where data is processed

Our servers are in Indonesia. The third-party services listed above may process data in other countries. We only share what each one needs.

Children

ApeX is not for anyone under 18, and we don’t knowingly collect their data. If you believe a minor uses ApeX, contact us and we will remove the account.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. For significant changes we will give notice in the bot or ApeX Pro.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests: support@apexutility.fun. See also our Terms of Service.
